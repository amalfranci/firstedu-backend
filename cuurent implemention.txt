Condition to even apply these: tier === "hard" || examCalibrated (validateHardQuestionMandate). Theory-kind and direct-kind slots get relaxed rules (theory just needs ≥2 reasoning steps; direct slots skip the concept-fusion/step-count gates entirely since they're meant to be single-technique).

B. src\services\difficultySelfAudit.service.js — a separate LLM call scores the skeleton/question 0–100 against a rubric, and anything below the floor is rejected/regenerated:

export const DIFFICULTY_SELF_AUDIT_MIN_SCORE = Number(
    process.env.AI_QB_DIFFICULTY_SELF_AUDIT_MIN ||
        (isVeteranDifficultyEnabled() ? 80 : 75)
);
export const SKELETON_DIFFICULTY_SELF_AUDIT_MIN_SCORE = Number(
    process.env.AI_QB_SKELETON_DIFFICULTY_SELF_AUDIT_MIN ||
        (isVeteranDifficultyEnabled() ? 78 : 70)
);
(That 78 is exactly the minScore: 78 you saw in the logs — skeleton-stage floor, veteran mode.) There's also a relaxed floor (72) if most of a batch would otherwise get wiped, and a last-attempt floor (55) so a batch never ends at 0.

C. src\services\examGenerationDifficulty.service.js — decides difficulty before any of the above runs at all: if the topic/bank looks like a real entrance exam (JEE Main/Advanced, NEET, CAT — detected via isExamPaperGenerationContext), it ignores the UI's Easy/Medium/Hard setting entirely and forces generationDifficulty = "hard" for every question, tagging the batch examCalibrated: true (this is the EXAM_NATIVE_DIFFICULTY / "exam-native" logic you saw in every run's logs).

In one sentence: exam-native resolution decides whether everything must be hard → the hard-mandate service enforces hard structurally (concepts/steps/lines/no-plug-in) at write time → the difficulty self-audit LLM call double-checks it qualitatively (0–100 score) before it's accepted.
